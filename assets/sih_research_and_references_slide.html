💡
  • Cognitive Decline & Dementia in India: Over 8.8 million Indians aged 60+ live with dementia (projected to reach 16.9 million by 2036, Lancet / LASI Report). Early non-pharmacological digital intervention slows cognitive decline and preserves independence.
  • Clinical Evidence for Memory Training: Active recall exercises, spatial sequencing, and autobiographical memory cues stimulate neuroplasticity and improve delayed recall by up to 28% (WHO Guidelines on Risk Reduction of Cognitive Decline).
  • Digital Accessibility & Elder-Tech Gap: NITI Aayog's Senior Care Economy Report (2024) highlights an acute lack of regional vernacular UI, voice-guided interfaces, and offline resilience in existing digital healthcare tools.
Full Details & Literature: WHO Guidelines (2023) • Alzheimer’s Association • NITI Aayog Senior Care Report • Lancet Neurology (LASI Study)
⚠️ SOLUTIONS ALREADY EXIST
  • Complex & Overwhelming: Cluttered UI, tiny text, and rapid timers unsuited for elderly seniors.
  • Impersonal Brain Puzzles: Generic abstract math/speed drills, zero connection to personal family memories or life story.
  • Isolated Experience: No caregiver involvement, emotional monitoring, or emergency safety fallback.
  • Language Barrier: English-only with no regional vernacular speech or Indian cultural context.
AVAILABLE APPS : Lumosity / Elevate / Peak / BrainHQ
➔
✨ OUR SOLUTION STANDS OUT
  • Elder-Centric Accessibility: Large touch buttons (≥48px), high contrast, calm peach/cream palette, and zero time pressure.
  • Autobiographical Memory Cues: Integrates personal family photos, voice memories, and familiar loved ones recognition.
  • Multilingual Voice Guidance: Hands-free voice navigation and text-to-speech in English, Hindi, and Bengali.
  • Caregiver Ecosystem: Automated emotional trend tracking, shared weekly goals, and remote medication alerts.
  • 100% Offline PWA Resilience: Works seamlessly in rural areas without continuous high-speed internet.
